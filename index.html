Selected projects & case studies

A few examples of how satellite data and climate analytics have been used in real projects. Full case studies can be shared on request.

Punjab · Wheat & climate risk
Client type: Government / research collaboration

Wheat–Climate Impact Assessment (2004–2030)

Combined crop statistics, satellite-derived climate indicators and emissions data to estimate long- and short-term impacts of climate variability on district-level wheat yields.

Deliverables: Maps, climate–yield elasticities, forecast scenarios, policy notes.

Punjab & Sindh · Rice mapping
Client type: Food security project

Rice Area Estimation via NDVI/NDWI Curves

Used Landsat and Sentinel-2 NDVI/NDWI time series with agro-climatic zoning to detect rice areas, improving estimates beyond conventional reporting.

Deliverables: Classification GeoTIFFs, district-wise area tables, methodology report.

Karakoram · Glacier hazards
Client type: Disaster risk & climate adaptation

Snow & Glacier Hazard Zonation (Baltoro–Siachen)

Integrated DEM-derived slope, snow cover change and satellite backscatter to identify zones with elevated avalanche and melt-related hazard.

Deliverables: Hazard maps, change statistics and recommendations for monitoring.

About Muhammad Hassan

GIS and remote sensing specialist focused on agriculture, climate change and glacier hazards, with extensive experience across Pakistan’s provinces and high-mountain regions.

I work at the intersection of remote sensing, climate science and applied statistics. Using tools such as Google Earth Engine, ArcGIS, QGIS, Python and advanced econometric models, I build workflows that turn large volumes of satellite and climate data into practical insights for decision makers.

My work includes long-term crop–climate analyses, district-level crop area estimation, and glacier hazard studies in some of the most data-sparse regions. I collaborate with government agencies, research teams and projects that need reliable, technically robust geospatial evidence.
